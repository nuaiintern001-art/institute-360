ABOUT THE COLLEGE:
Rajalakshmi Institute of Technology is an autonomous engineering institution established in 2008. It is affiliated with Anna University and approved by AICTE. The college has NAAC A++ accreditation.

UNDERGRADUATE PROGRAMMES:
1. Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
2. Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI & ML)
3. Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (AI & DS)
4. Computer and Communication Engineering (CCE)
5. Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)
6. Mechanical Engineering
7. Biotechnology
8. VLSI Design and Technology
9. Computer Science and Business Systems (CSBS)

ACADEMIC FACILITIES:
The institution provides laboratories, classrooms, research facilities and Future Tech Centres covering areas such as:
- Artificial Intelligence
- Cloud Computing and Cybersecurity
- Internet of Things
- Augmented and Virtual Reality
- Data Science
- Advanced Manufacturing
- Electric Vehicles
